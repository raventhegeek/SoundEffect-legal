# Sound Effects: Voice Changer legal pages

Public privacy policy and terms of use for PerfectSky Studios' Android app.

- `index.html`: privacy policy, English and Turkish
- `terms-of-service.html`: terms of use, English and Turkish
- `styles.css`: shared legal-page layout used by the studio's other apps, with SoundEffect colors
- `app_logo.png` and `studio_logo.jpg`: app and publisher marks

The pages are intended for GitHub Pages at `https://raventhegeek.github.io/SoundEffect-legal/`.
