Privacy Policy

English

1. Scope and publisher

Sound Effects: Voice Changer is published by PerfectSky Studios. No account is required. The app processes audio you select or record on your device; we do not operate a server that receives your audio.

2. Audio, storage and permissions

Imported clips, recordings, waveforms, projects, effect settings and exports are stored locally in app-private storage. Microphone access is requested only when you start recording. Android's file picker grants access to files you choose. The app does not request broad storage or location permission.

3. Advertising and consent

The free app uses Google AdMob and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). AdMob may collect and share IP addresses, app interactions, diagnostics and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. UMP manages applicable consent choices; where required, you can revisit them in the app's Settings. Our app code does not send audio content, file names or project data to ad requests. See Google's Privacy Policy for Google's practices.

4. Purchases and sharing

Pro purchases are not currently available. If enabled later, Google Play will handle payment and the app will check purchase status; the app will not receive card details. Saving an export to shared device storage or sending it to another app happens when you choose that action. The receiving app then handles the shared copy under its own terms.

5. Retention and your choices

Local projects and exports remain until you remove them in the app or clear app data. Uninstalling removes app-private data but may leave copies you saved outside the app; delete those with a file manager. App backup is disabled. Android settings provide microphone and advertising-ID controls. You can contact us about privacy questions or applicable data requests.

6. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its services change and will revise the effective date. Contact perfectskystudios@gmail.com.
